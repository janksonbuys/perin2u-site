Sourcing from China is easy. Sourcing from the right factory — one whose quality holds on the second container, not just the first — is the part that costs people money.

Who we are

A sensor specialist, not a general trader.

PERIN2U is the sensor brand of Wenzhou Perin Trading Co., Ltd., based in Wenzhou, Zhejiang. We do one category: exhaust gas sensors — oxygen, NOx and ammonia — for the aftermarket.

That focus is deliberate. A general trader can quote you anything and stand behind nothing. We would rather know every part number we sell, where it is made, which vehicle it fits and what usually goes wrong with it.

We are also not a factory, and we do not describe ourselves as one. Our value is on the other side of that line: we choose the line, we check what comes off it, and we are the ones you call when something is wrong.

How we work

Verify first. Ship second.

The most expensive mistake in parts distribution is not a bad price. It is a wrong application: freight both ways, labour twice, a bay blocked, and a customer who stops calling.

So the order of operations is fixed. Application first. Sample and function test second. Packaging and documentation third. Container last. If a number cannot be verified, it does not go on the list — even if the customer asked for it by name.

  • Sensor programmes run with factories certified to IATF 16949 — certificate sets available for your audit file
  • 100% function test before shipment, with a batch record we keep
  • Packaging, labelling and barcodes set for the destination market
  • Factory audits arranged and attended on request

Our name, our method

PERIN2U is not a label. It is a commitment read left to right.

Per — PersistenceQuality in this category is not claimed — it is accumulated. Hand samples, engineering samples, small-batch field trials, then volume. We run every programme the slow way on purpose, because that is the only way quality survives real SCR duty.
in — InsistenceThis category has been burned before — by surplus stock hiding behind borrowed production lines. Our rule is absolute: if a product has not completed the validation path, we do not sell it, no matter how attractive the price.
2U — For youChoosing the wrong sensor supplier costs months of returns, warranty claims and lost customers. We exist to absorb that cost for you — so selecting the right product and supplier costs you nothing but a phone call.

Our earliest customers started with pilot quantities years ago. They are the ones increasing orders now. In this category, trust compounds with time — that is the entire business model.
