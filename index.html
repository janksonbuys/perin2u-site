About PERIN2U
Questions purchasing teams ask first

Straight answers

Are you a manufacturer?
No — and we don't pretend to be. We are a sensor specialist that buys direct from Chinese production lines and takes responsibility for what leaves the factory. If your process requires a factory audit, we arrange it and attend it with you.
What is your MOQ?
Flexible for a first order. We would rather prove ourselves on a pilot programme than lock you into volume before you have seen a single return rate. Mixed-model consolidations are normal for us.
Can you deliver fitment data in ACES or PIES format?
Yes — we deliver a structured ACES 5.0 file covering 10,853 part applications and 30,572 vehicle fitments. Before a programme starts we tell you exactly which fields are complete and which are not, because we would rather flag a gap than have your catalogue team discover it during import.
Do you support private label?
Yes. Packaging, part numbering and data files under your own brand, with the ACES/PIES interchange built so your customers can still cross-reference the OE number.
How do you handle a warranty claim?
Engineer-led review within one business day. If the failure was a mis-diagnosis rather than the part, we say so and show you why. If it was the part, you get a replacement and a written failure report.
Which markets do you ship to?
Europe, North America, the Middle East and Africa, with documentation, labelling and packaging adapted per market.
